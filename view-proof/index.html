WHAT CHANGED

Pixels with a common scale

The new sheet uses longer bodies, smaller heads and simpler faces. Its display frames share a 32-pixel height, crisp square pixels and one 48-colour palette. The current cast is available for a direct comparison.

WHAT COMES NEXT

Animation and layered outfits

This is a standing-pose style candidate. Walking, blinking, work poses and interchangeable hair, shirts and hats still need matching artwork and their own checks before this cast can enter the game.

This isolated review reads no story save and writes no progress. The new cast is a generated sheet, normalised into 24×32 display cells with nearest-neighbour sampling and palette matching. It is not a hand-cleaned production sprite set. The cottage and ground are art trials. The approved dialogue portraits and the live game stay unchanged.

On a narrow screen the camera shows a smaller part of the scene. Start at 2× to see all three characters together; 4× centres on the player and crops the outer cast. Keyboard: 1–4 changes direction; Space pauses or plays when the canvas has focus.
