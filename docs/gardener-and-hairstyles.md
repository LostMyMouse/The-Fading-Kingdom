# Rowan, a new neighbour · 0.9.0

Restore the nursery to meet Rowan. Invitation is optional. Place a separate home freely, choose one of three styles and fund three physical construction stages at the sign (12 G + 16 G + 12 G). Mill supplies use village gold rather than exhausting the original finite loose resources. The finished home has its own accessible interior and automatic doorway. Rowan tends nursery pots, checks plants and takes breaks independently; room-local routines are retained, while Orin’s existing continuous breakfast visit still works.

Collect a one-time gift of three turnip and three marigold seeds in the shop. Swap two carrots for three turnip seeds, or one carrot for three marigold seeds. Both need two watered nights, retain growth through reload and missed watering, and use the original nine garden plots. Carrots and free starter seeds stay available. The seed shop contributes 6 G each morning.

Growing two marigolds for the village path earns a row-sowing attachment. Choose a crop in Seeds and plant up to three empty patches in the same row, spending one seed per patch. Existing plants are never replaced. Earned equipment remains available during disagreements.

A tagged seed-delivery parcel can be picked up without an immediate accusation. Return it unopened, or open it for three turnip seeds. Missing deliveries reduce only seed-shop contributions to 2 G from the next morning. Return the parcel or cover a new one with one grown turnip at the counter to restore tomorrow’s full contribution. No repeat reward or global morality score is added.

Character creator: Tousled, Tied back, Rounded bob and Twin puffs. Saved legacy style 2 remains its earlier tousled alias; new options use IDs 3 and 4. Hair, skin and clothing palettes, shirts and cap remain compatible. Mara keeps her low tied hair, Orin his cap, Rowan has copper curls; original approved portrait atlases are unchanged. New Rowan art matches those references and is not a replacement style authority.

Guided walking avoids unrelated building thresholds; manually walking into a doorway still enters automatically. This corrects an issue found when the path from the nursery to Rowan’s construction site crossed the bakery entrance.

Deferred: carried-item attachment, more wardrobe, chopping/mining tools, continuous cross-room routines for Mara/Rowan and a full day/night system. The cutout animation rig remains a prototype.
