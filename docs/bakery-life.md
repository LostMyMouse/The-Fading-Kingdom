# A lived-in bakery · 0.8.0

Orin follows a persistent breakfast errand: walks from his cart to the player-placed bakery door, enters the separate kitchen, buys breakfast, takes a break, leaves and returns to work. His journey continues when the player views another room; room changes do not restart him. His route waits for occupied tiles, pauses for conversation, respects reduced motion, and never rewards waiting or repeats income. The morning settlement remains the economy authority. Short in-world exchanges are decorative; speaking to Orin still opens his normal dialogue.

After a full bakery day, Mara can ask for two carrots for Orin’s workshop lunch. Agree or decline naturally; declining changes nothing. Bring the carrots to the actual preparation counter. If the next batch arrives first, she makes a plain loaf and the request remains open. Delivering two carrots prepares the tray; the next full batch adds a one-time 4 G lunch contribution. A flour shortage delays the tray rather than duplicating it or destroying ingredients. Mara and Orin remember the completed shared job in everyday conversation.

After the lunch and two full bakery days, a 16 G village project adds flowering window boxes and warm window/door lights to the existing freely placed bakery. Orin supplies old offcuts; no further finite gathering stock is needed. The improvement has no additional collision footprint and does not change the map or existing decorations. These are warm bakery lights, not a new day/night simulation.

Existing saves migrate with no invented order or relationship memories. No personal friendship meter, forced gifting, or accumulating chat reward is added. Visits are transient presentation state and reset at a new session/day; completed promises and projects persist. Public-market trade remains unchanged.

Deferred: carried-item/hand alignment (including the reported sideways basket), additional wardrobe options, fully authored production sprite frames, more villagers/businesses and a true day/night system. No character or portrait raster assets are changed in this release.
