# Village economy and repairable setbacks · 0.7.0

The village treasury funds both homes/businesses and shared improvements. There is no personal coin balance or morality score in this chapter.

## Playable loop
The village begins with a 12 G welcome fund. Finishing Mara’s home spends that once, alongside the existing wood/stone construction stages. Her first cooked batch opens the bakery. Each night’s sleep settles one day of trade, once per day even across saves: 10 G for a full bakery batch, 4 G if the reserved order flour is missing. The market adds 4 G in either case. No offline arrears, forced sharing or polite-phrase bonus.

The player chooses either project first:
- Nursery: 18 G, 4 wood, 2 stone. Restores the existing nursery site. From the following day, pick two berries once per day. After at least one full bakery day, Mara can share her fruit-bun recipe. Two berries and one carrot make three fruit buns through the existing mix/shape/bake interaction.
- Market stall: 24 G, 4 wood, 2 stone. Opens west of the garden, adds passing trade and an independent breakfast errand for Orin. It cannot overwrite decorations or trap a player standing on the site. Both projects together fit the loose materials left after Mara’s house.

Public projects use fixed existing village sites in this first pass. Mara’s home still uses free placement. Further freely placed homes/businesses can use the same treasury in later chapters; they are not implemented here.

## Taking without asking
After the bakery opens, a useful flour sack sits on the kitchen shelf. Its tag says Morning orders. Pick up the sack is a neutral action: no instant portrait, accusation, journal warning or lost money. It can be returned unchanged or used to bake three simple rolls. The smaller batch and supply discovery occur at the next sleep/morning settlement. Bread displays shrink and Mara comments on the missing supply. New recipe-sharing waits while the sack is missing; previously earned recipes and tools remain available.

Return the original sack to restore tomorrow’s full batch. If it was used, exchange two carrots with Orin for a replacement and physically put it on the kitchen shelf. Orin’s mill delivery can be reached even during an unrelated boards/basket issue. There is no repeat pickup/reward loop, and repairing the supply does not refund already missed sales. Farming, seeds, decorating, the original cooking recipe and unrelated relationships remain available. A reduced contribution still allows gradual village progress.

## Save compatibility
The Chapter One save key remains unchanged. Missing economy and berry/fruit-bun fields migrate without changing existing story/appearance/inventory values. Existing completed homes are recorded as already supported by the welcome fund, with no retroactive bakery income. New ledgers retain the latest eight entries. Preview playtests use a separate save key and do not overwrite the user’s story.

## Scope
This is one concrete cooperation chain, not a universal social simulation. Repeated rude responses, garden damage, broader trust memories and additional independent businesses still need authored situations. Expanded hairstyles, shirts and hats remain deferred at James’s request.
