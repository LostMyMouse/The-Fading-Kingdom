# Mara and the riverside · 0.13.0 · 24 October 2026

This extends the production chapter's existing first-resident story. It does not create another baker or replace the approved cast. Mara's home remains freely placed, her kitchen remains enterable, and the cottage/environment art walkthrough stays separate from the full production-map treatment.

## Playable loop

Grow carrots → meet Mara → choose and build her home with Orin → cook the first tray → the bakery contributes village gold when the player sleeps → reserve shared footbridge supplies → build with Orin → walk across the existing pond to a small island → pick wild berries and use the existing fruit-bun recipe.

The journal and village fund now offer “Mara and the riverside”, a four-part recap drawn only from saved events. Reading it never awards money, raises a score, chooses a response or discloses an unnoticed ownership issue. The normal first-hour guidance and other project choices remain available. Once bridge supplies are reserved, guidance can help find Orin.

## Shared project and consequences

The bridge uses 24 village gold, six stored wood and four stored stone. Costs are shown before confirmation. Carried supplies do not count until deposited. Funds/materials are spent once when reserved; finishing or reloading never charges again. A blocked bank approach prevents spending. Supplies wait if Orin needs his borrowed basket or mill boards returned. Repair is practical and local; no permanent exile, confiscated equipment or invented moral verdict is added.

The existing flour interaction remains an ordinary pickup. A missing order sack affects the next bakery batch and shared income, which can delay reaching the bridge budget. Returning/replacing it restores subsequent full trading days; it does not mint compensation. An unrepaired shortage still allows eventual bridge progress, basic farming and previously earned equipment.

## Real access and village life

The map remains 56×40. The bridge and island use the existing pond bounds. The island is unreachable until construction completes; surrounding water stays blocked. The berry bush has a collision tile with an accessible interaction spot. New decoration cannot obstruct the paid bank approach, crossing or berry access, but other island ground can be decorated if essential paths remain reachable. Existing homes/decorations are not relocated or overwritten.

The patch gives two berries per game day through a physical visit. Repeated interactions/reloads do not duplicate it; no offline payout is added. These are the existing berry inventory items and fruit-bun recipe. Mara gains an independently scheduled lunch stop on the island after completion; proximity does not steer her.

## Saves and review

The production save key/version and existing dimensions remain. Missing bridge data migrate to an unbuilt bridge, with no retroactive income or item grants. New bridge flags/days and bridge ledger entries are validated. Main-game progress, appearance, plots, buildings and furniture are preserved.

Use `?review=bridge` for a separate playtest slot, `the-fading-kingdom:bridge-review:v1`. Other query values continue using the production slot. Test checkpoints are generated by the production rules journey and restored through the normal backup UI in that review slot; they are not the user's story. The profile does not read the production save or earlier prototype save.
